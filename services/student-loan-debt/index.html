If student loan repayments or IRD debt have become hard to manage, getting the right advice early can make a big difference.

At Meridian Partners, we help you deal with Inland Revenue, reduce pressure, and work towards a clear plan to get things back on track.

Clear, Practical Help With IRD Issues

Falling behind on student loan repayments or tax obligations can happen for many reasons — time overseas, business cashflow issues, or unexpected life events.

What matters is what you do next.

We work with you to understand your situation and deal directly with Inland Revenue on your behalf. Our goal is to reduce stress, avoid further penalties, and find a solution that is workable long term.

Student Loan Debt Negotiation

If your student loan balance has grown due to missed repayments, late payment interest, or time spent overseas, we can help you take control again.

We assist with:

  • Negotiating manageable repayment plans

  • Applying for repayment relief based on your circumstances

  • Reducing penalties and interest where possible

  • Communicating with IRD on your behalf

  • Structuring payments that match your income and cashflow

Whether you are back in New Zealand or still overseas, we can help you move forward with a clear plan.

IRD Debt & Tax Penalty Negotiation

If you have outstanding tax, penalties, or interest with Inland Revenue, early action is key.

We can help with:

  • Negotiating repayment arrangements with IRD

  • Applying for remission of penalties and interest

  • Resolving overdue tax returns

  • Assisting with IRD disputes and reviews

  • Managing communication to avoid further escalation

Our focus is on getting a fair outcome and putting a plan in place that you can stick to.

Reduce Pressure and Avoid Escalation

Ignoring IRD debt can lead to:

  • Ongoing penalties and interest

  • Legal action or enforcement

  • Credit and financial pressure

We step in early to help prevent things from getting worse and to give you clarity around your options.

A Practical, Straightforward Approach

We keep things simple:

  • Clear advice on where you stand

  • Realistic options based on your situation

  • Direct negotiation with IRD

  • Fixed fee options available for many services

No surprises. Just a clear path forward.
