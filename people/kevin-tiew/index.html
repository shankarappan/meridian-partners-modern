Present role

Kevin Tiew is a solicitor at Meridian Partners. He advises property owners, investors, developers, business owners and private companies on property and commercial matters.

Relevant experience

Kevin holds an LLB. His work includes identifying the legal and commercial issues in proposed transactions, reviewing documentation and advising clients on the options available.

Principal areas of work

  • Residential and commercial property transactions
  • Business acquisitions and disposals
  • Commercial leases
  • Business and asset ownership arrangements
  • Private and commercial financing
  • Trust matters

Working across practices

Kevin works with the firm’s property and commercial practice, involving other Meridian lawyers where a transaction raises tax, regulatory or dispute issues.

Professional memberships

  • New Zealand Law Society
  • Property Law Section, New Zealand Law Society
