Solicitor

Ivan Ng

Property, commercial and trusts

Contact through info@mplaw.nz

Present role

Ivan Ng is a solicitor at Meridian Partners with a practice focused on property, commercial and trust matters. He advises individuals and businesses through a solution-orientated approach.

Relevant experience

Admitted as a lawyer in 2021, Ivan began his legal career in an in-house role within a Crown entity, where he developed experience in commercial negotiations. He subsequently entered private practice and has developed experience advising on a broad range of property, commercial and trust issues.

Ivan holds a Bachelor of Laws (Honours) from the University of Liverpool and is currently completing a Master of Business Management at Auckland University of Technology.

Principal areas of work

  • Residential and commercial property acquisitions and sales
  • Commercial leasing
  • Due diligence, ownership structuring and asset protection
  • Commercial contracts
  • Trust administration, estate planning and wills

Approach to matters

Ivan takes an approachable, down-to-earth and strategic approach. He is detail-oriented and focuses on practical solutions for clients.
